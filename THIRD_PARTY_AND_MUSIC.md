# Third-party / music notes — v9

This repository does **not** bundle Nintendo / Rhythm Heaven graphics, music, voice clips, text, logos, or extracted game data.

Optional external tracks are CC0 items from OpenGameArt. Their source URLs and runtime filenames are documented in `THIRD_PARTY_AND_RESEARCH.md` and `scripts/fetch-cc0-music.mjs`.

The default/fallback music is generated at runtime with Web Audio and is original to this project. Cue sounds and the included `hey.wav`, `go.wav`, `yeah.wav` one-shots are project assets, not extracted from commercial games.
